Access Control — Overview

This section provides the high-level model and pointers for Workspace, Space, and Project access control. Use this as your starting point, then dive into the dedicated pages.

Overview

  • Business Goal: Ensure the right users have the right level of access to the right content at the right scope (Workspace → Space → Project).
  • Scopes: Workspace (org boundary), Space (team/product area), Project (boards/tasks under a Space).
  • Roles: Each scope defines roles (e.g., Owner, Admin, Member, Guest/Viewer) that govern read/write/admin actions.
  • Invitation model: Invitations use an invitation_id (UUID) for acceptance; acceptance validates the invitee’s email against the authenticated user.
  • Discoverability: Content is private to members unless explicitly shared; invite codes may allow controlled self-join at the workspace level.
  • Auditing: Track invitations, acceptances, role changes, removals for compliance and support.
  • Non-goals: Unauthenticated public access to Workspace/Space/Project content.

Architecture Summary

  • Identity: GoTrue issues JWTs; AppFlowy verifies and maps to internal users.
  • Authorization: Role/permission checks at Workspace, Space, and Project layers; enforced in handlers and reflected in the UI.
  • Inheritance: Space and Project may derive or override from higher scopes; see each page for details.

Cross-cutting Concerns

  • Auditing & Logs: Record membership changes and access denials.
  • Caching: Consider permission caching with invalidation on role changes.
  • Feature Flags: Gate new ACL features; document env toggles.
